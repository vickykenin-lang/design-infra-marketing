Privacy Policy

Contact information you share with us is used solely to respond to your inquiry, provide quotes, and deliver our interior design services. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Social Media & Third-Party Platforms

Our Instagram and Pinterest accounts are managed in part by automated publishing tools we operate. These tools post content on our behalf and do not access, store, or process personal data belonging to other users of those platforms.

Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies for standard functionality. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising tracking.
