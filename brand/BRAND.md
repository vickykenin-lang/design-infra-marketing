# Design Infra — Brand Book v1
*Defined by AURA, Day 2. Owner approval pending on public-facing identity elements.*

## Positioning (one line)
**"एक टीम। एक कॉन्ट्रैक्ट। आपका पूरा घर।"** — True turnkey interiors for India's metro homeowners, delivered by one accountable team from design to handover.

## Target audience
- **Primary:** Homeowners & flat buyers, 28–45, in Delhi NCR (then Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad), mid-to-premium budgets, planning full-home or office interiors.
- **Secondary:** NRIs furnishing homes in Indian metros; business owners planning office fit-outs.
- **Mindset we target:** "I want a beautiful home but I'm terrified of contractor chaos, hidden costs, and endless follow-ups."

## Brand voice
- **Feel:** Warm-premium. An expert friend, not a salesman. Confident, transparent, never pushy.
- **Instagram:** Hinglish — English hook + Hindi warmth. Short sentences. One idea per post.
- **Pinterest:** Clean English, search-optimized (Pinterest is a search engine).
- **Always:** honest labels on AI concept images ("Concept visualisation"), real numbers where possible, no fake urgency, no "DM now!!!" spam tone.

## Visual style
- Palette: warm ivory `#faf7f2`, charcoal `#1f2937`, terracotta/amber accent `#b45309 → #d97706` (same family as dashboard — one brand everywhere).
- Typography: bold clean sans for hooks; generous whitespace; consistent card frames.
- Image order of preference: 1) real project photos (when owner supplies), 2) programmatic layout cards (quote/tips/cost cards), 3) AI concept renders — always labeled "Concept".

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
1. **Design Tips & Mistakes** — 30% (saves & shares → reach)
2. **Before/After & Concept Transformations** — 25% (desire)
3. **Budget & Process Transparency** — 20% (trust; highest lead intent)
4. **Trends & Festive/Seasonal** — 15% (relevance: Independence Day, Raksha Bandhan, Ganesh Chaturthi, Diwali, wedding season)
5. **Turnkey Education & Brand Story** — 10% (conversion: why one team beats ten contractors)

## Cadence (start conservative, scale on data)
- Instagram: 1 post/day at 19:00 IST (evening scroll peak). Scale to 2/day only after 2 green weeks.
- Pinterest: 1–2 pins/day (evergreen, SEO-first descriptions).
- Every post ends with one soft CTA: "Free consultation — link in bio."

## Differentiators (approved Day 1)
1. एक टीम, ज़ीरो झंझट — one contract, not ten contractors
2. Delhi NCR craftsmanship, pan-India metro delivery
3. Milestone-based transparency — client approves every stage
4. Design-first — projects start with designers, not salespeople
